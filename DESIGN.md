# Design System: TripMate
**Project ID:** 1674063069447220901

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
TripMate's design is "Scholar-Explorer" centric—clean, academic yet adventurous, and highly legible. It balances the airy, minimalist feel of white surface areas with high-density informational zones (itineraries and maps). The atmosphere is "Premium Utilitarian," prioritizing functionality without sacrificing the emotional pull of travel.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Solar Amber (#F59E0B)**: Primary action color. Used for CTA buttons, "AI-Powered" badges, and active navigation indicators. Represents intelligence and energy.
*   **Deep Explorer Blue (#1E3A8A)**: Brand/Logo color. Used for wordmarks, high-emphasis headers, and "Start Planning" buttons. Represents depth and reliability.
*   **Alabaster White (#FFFFFF)**: Primary surface background. Used for cards, modals, and the main dashboard background.
*   **Ink Gray (#111827)**: Primary text color. Used for headings and high-contrast body text.
*   **Emerald Horizon (#10B981)**: Accent color for success states, progress indicators, and active toggles.
*   **Mist Overlay (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2))**: Semi-transparent glassmorphism layer used for buttons placed over imagery.

## 3. Typography Rules
*   **Font Family**: `Inter`, optimized for UI legibility.
*   **Headings**: Semi-bold to Bold (600-700) with tight letter-spacing. H1s are large and impactful (3.0rem), while section headers (H2s) are balanced (1.875rem).
*   **Body**: Regular weight (400) with a generous line-height (1.5) for readability during long-form itinerary reading.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons**: Generously padded (`py-3 px-6`) with `rounded-xl` (12px) corners. Primary buttons use a solid Solar Amber fill.
*   **Cards/Containers**: Large, soft-form containers with `rounded-3xl` (24px) corners. Surfaces are clean white with subtle `shadow-md` elevation.
*   **Navigation**: Bottom tab bar (mobile) or persistent sidebar (desktop) with elevated action buttons and backdrop-blur effects.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **White Space**: Strategic use of margins and gutters to avoid informational density fatigue.
*   **Mobile-First Grid**: Highly responsive layout that stacks components logically on smaller screens.
*   **Layering**: Use of backdrop-blur and semi-transparent overlays to maintain text legibility over travel photography.
